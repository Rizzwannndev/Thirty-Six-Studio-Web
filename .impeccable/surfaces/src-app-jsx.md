## Direction contract

THESIS: One pepper leaves the hero swarm and becomes the page's camera. Each section is a set it moves through: it lands, performs and moves on. The page refuses the agency default of stacked fade-up sections and card grids.

OWN-WORLD: White paper ground, flooded to #fd2c2a red by a circle from the point of the click. Ink is pure #000, as on the original site, and nothing else. The material is real pepper image sequences on canvas. One grotesk family does everything, from 11rem wordmark to 14px body. Circles that grow from the point of action are the only transition shape (flood, menu, buttons). No cards, no gradients, no eyebrows.

STORY: The visitor sees the studio and then makes it come alive. They follow the pepper through manifesto, services, work, process and a hands-on playground. They end by writing to the studio.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Original layout kept. The nav is a thin row, the intro paragraph sits top-left at 2.4rem, and the wordmark "Thirtysixstudios" spans the full width along the bottom edge. A cursor label invites the click. After the flood, nine pepper canvases scatter across the viewport and the camera pepper is the largest, at the right.

FORM: Structure 3 of 7 on the ordered list: "The pepper is the camera". seed:551876bdebeac12b. Signature interaction: the travelling camera pepper, ending in the playground where the visitor scrubs its frames by hand. Motion grammar: expo.out for arrivals, expo.inOut for scene changes, scrub for anything tied to the scroll.
